PEN & PINScan to play on your Android phone

Point your camera here

This page is on your own computer, so a phone cannot open the code. Put the game on a web address first (see the README), or type your computer's network address below.
  1. Open the camera on your Android phone and scan the code. Tap the link that appears. It opens in Chrome.
  2. In Chrome tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open Pen & Pin from your home screen. It plays full screen, sideways, and starts offline too.

It is a web app, so there is no Play Store download and nothing else to install. Online matches need a connection; solo play works without one.
