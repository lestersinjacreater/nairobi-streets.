## Full description
Pen & Pin is a first-person shooter drawn entirely in blue ballpoint on lined notebook paper. Swing across
rooftops with your grappling hook, slice enemy bullets back at the doodles that fired them, throw grenades that
scorch the page, and see how many waves you can survive.

SOLO SURVIVAL
- Wave after wave of ink-drawn enemies: grunts, rushers, bombers, snipers with dodgeable lasers, flyers, heavies and shield bearers.
- A boss every fifth wave, each with its own moves.
- Checkpoints unlock as you get further, so you can start from a wave you have reached.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
- Free for all with up to ten players, first to 20 kills.
- Quick Play joins an open lobby, or make a private lobby and share the five-letter code.
- Players can join a match that is already running.

YOUR TOOLKIT
- Rifle, shotgun, sniper with a scope, katana and boxing gloves.
- Grapple hook: swing, reel in, launch, and even ride the paper planes flying over the city.
- Katana block that parries bullets and sends them back.
- Grenades you can hold to throw further, wall jumps, double jumps, slides and air dashes.

MADE FOR PHONES
- Install it and it plays full screen, sideways, with touch controls built for thumbs: a big fire button, a second fire button by the stick, tap-to-scope, and buttons for jump, slide, hook, grenade and weapon swap.
- A paired Bluetooth controller works too.
- Solo play works without an internet connection.

THE LOOK
Everything is drawn in code: the paper, the ink outlines, the hatching, the enemies and the 8-bit music. There are no photos or stock art, just a notebook that fights back.

No ads. No accounts. No in-app purchases.

Contains cartoon-style violence (shooting hand-drawn stick figures). Online play connects you directly to other players; see the privacy policy for details.
