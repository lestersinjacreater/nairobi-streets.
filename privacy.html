Privacy Policy

Pen & Pin · effective 14 October 2026

The short version: Pen & Pin has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect or sell your personal information. Your settings stay on your own device. When you play online, the other players in your match can see your display name and connect to your device directly.

Who we are

Pen & Pin is made by Lester Sinja Creator. You can reach us at lestetsinjawatosia@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not run a game server or a database, and we do not ask you to sign up. The game has no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things in your browser or app storage so it remembers you next time:

  • your display name (a random name unless you change it)
  • your settings (sensitivity, music, invert look, selected map)
  • your best score and your wave checkpoint
  • a copy of the game files, so it can start without a connection

This never leaves your device. Clearing the site data, or uninstalling the app, deletes it.

Playing online

Online matches are peer to peer: your device connects directly to the other players, and the player who made the lobby keeps the score. To make that possible:

  • Other players in your lobby see your display name and what your character does in the match. Because the devices connect directly, they can also see your network (IP) address, as with any peer-to-peer game. Please do not use your real name as a display name.
  • Matchmaking service: a public PeerJS server (peerjs.com) introduces players to each other. It receives a random connection id and your network address while you look for or join a lobby.
  • Connection helpers: public STUN servers (Google, Cloudflare) help devices find each other, and a relay service (Open Relay by Metered) may carry the game traffic when a direct connection is not possible.

These services have their own privacy practices and we do not control them. If you do not want to use them, play solo, which needs no connection at all. There is no text chat or voice chat in the game.

Hosting

The game files are served from a web host (Vercel). Like any website, the host may keep standard server logs, such as your network address and the time of the request, for security and to run the service.

Children

Pen & Pin has cartoon-style violence and is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. Because we collect nothing, there is nothing for us to delete, but if you have a concern, contact us.

Your choices

  • Change your display name in the game’s settings, or play solo to share nothing with anyone.
  • Delete everything the game stored by clearing the site data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts to use new services or collect new information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change goes live.

Contact
